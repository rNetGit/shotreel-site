How it works

Three steps from clicks to video

Record or import, write what each step says, then export. Nothing leaves the computer on the way.

1

Capture

Click through a web app and every click becomes a step with its box already placed. Or click through a Windows desktop app and the control you clicked is named for you.

2

Narrate

Type the words for each step, and a built-in offline English voice reads them. Or export the script, edit it with any AI or writer, and import it back.

3

Export

Get an MP4 at 720p or 1080p, captions as .srt and .vtt, and a step-guide PDF to print or attach to a ticket.

Prefer screenshots? Import them, or short screen clips, instead of capturing.

What it does

Everything a how-to needs, in one app

Capture

Every click becomes a step.

Record a web app or a Windows desktop app and ShotReel builds the step list for you, each with a screenshot and the highlight in the right place.

  • Web apps: a step per click, box already placed
  • Windows desktop apps: the clicked control is named for you
  • Or import screenshots and short screen clips you already have
Save
3
Annotate

Point at the thing that matters, hide the thing that does not.

Add boxes, arrows, spotlights, numbered badges and text notes, then zoom in on the part of the screen you are explaining. Blur or redact anything sensitive before it is rendered.

  • Boxes, arrows, spotlights, badges and text notes
  • Zoom to a region of the screen
  • Blur or redaction for customer or internal data
Narrate

A voice, without a microphone or a cloud.

Built-in English voices run on your PC. If you would rather have a person or an AI write the words, export the script, edit it anywhere and import it back.

  • Offline voices, nothing sent to a speech service
  • Export the script, edit it, import it back
  • Captions in .srt and .vtt

Export and reuse

A video, captions and a guide, from the same steps.

One project gives you an MP4 (720p or 1080p, no watermark), caption files and a printable PDF. When you change one step, only that part is rendered again, and a whole project shares as a single .shotreel file.

  • MP4, captions and PDF step guide
  • Unchanged parts are not re-rendered
  • Share a project as one .shotreel file

Price

Free, and staying free

The core features in this version stay free. Later versions may add paid extras for teams, such as 4K output, batch rendering and branding. Nothing you have today will be taken away.

Free

Use it at work

Free to use, including at work. You may not resell or redistribute ShotReel.

Needs

A normal Windows PC

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 2 GB of free disk space and 8 GB of RAM recommended. Rendering uses the CPU, so a longer video takes longer.

Size

About 650 MB installed

The voice model, renderer and video tools are bundled, so it works with no further downloads.
