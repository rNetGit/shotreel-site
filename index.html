How it works

Three steps from clicks to video

Record or import, write what each step says, then export. Nothing leaves the computer on the way.

1

Capture

Click through a web app and every click becomes a step with its box already placed. Or click through a Windows desktop app and the control you clicked is named for you.

2

Narrate

Type the words for each step, and a built-in offline English voice reads them. Or export the script, edit it with any AI or writer, and import it back.

3

Export

Get an MP4 at 720p or 1080p, or a 9:16 vertical cut for phones, captions as .srt and .vtt, and a step-guide PDF to print or attach to a ticket.

Prefer screenshots? Import them, or short screen clips, instead of capturing.

What it does

Everything a how-to needs, in one app

Capture

Every click becomes a step.

Record a web app or a Windows desktop app and ShotReel builds the step list for you, each with a screenshot and the highlight in the right place.

  • Web apps: a step per click, box already placed
  • Windows desktop apps: the clicked control is named for you
  • Or import screenshots and short screen clips you already have
Save
3
Annotate

Point at the thing that matters, hide the thing that does not.

Add boxes, arrows, spotlights, numbered badges and text notes, then zoom in on the part of the screen you are explaining. Password, email, phone, card and ID fields are blurred automatically when you capture, and you can blur anything else by hand.

  • Boxes, arrows, spotlights, badges and text notes
  • Zoom to a region of the screen
  • Sensitive fields blurred automatically at capture (you can turn it off per project)
  • Blur or redaction by hand for anything else
Narrate

A voice, without a microphone or a cloud.

Built-in English voices run on your PC. If you would rather have a person or an AI write the words, export the script, edit it anywhere and import it back. Demo mode adds a named guide who opens the video and hands over to your team at the end.

  • Offline voices, nothing sent to a speech service
  • Export the script, edit it, import it back
  • Demo mode: a named narrator, with "Narrated by an AI voice" always shown on the opening card
  • Captions in .srt and .vtt, or burned into the video

Export and reuse

A video, captions and a guide, from the same steps.

One project gives you an MP4 (720p, 1080p or 9:16 vertical, no watermark), caption files and a printable PDF. Mark the key steps and ShotReel cuts a 15, 30 or 60 second short. When you change one step, only that part is rendered again, and a whole project shares as a single .shotreel file.

  • MP4, captions and PDF step guide
  • 9:16 vertical and short clips for phones and chat
  • Intranet pack: video, captions, poster, PDF and a ready index.html page
  • Social pack: one MP4 per format, plus caption and hashtag text
  • Unchanged parts are not re-rendered
  • Share a project as one .shotreel file

What's new in 2.2.0

Phone-ready videos, safer captures, nothing online

9:16

Vertical and short clips

A 1080×1920 cut that follows each step, short clips capped at 15, 30 or 60 seconds, and Social and Intranet packs that put every file you need in one folder.

Blur

Sensitive fields hidden for you

Password, email, phone, card and ID fields are blurred as soon as a screen is captured, on the web and in Windows apps.

Offline

Rendering makes no network connections

Fonts are bundled and the renderer's usage reporting is switched off. Earlier versions (2.0.0 to 2.1.2) could contact an analytics service and download fonts while rendering when the PC was online; please update.

Also new: demo mode with a named AI narrator, a render progress bar, and security fixes from a full code review.

A normal Windows PC
