Questions

Plain answers

Cost

Is ShotReel free?

Yes. The free version has no time limit, no watermark and no project cap, and it is free to use at work.

Will it stay free?

The core features in this version stay free, and nothing you have today will be taken away. Later versions may add paid extras for teams, such as 4K output, batch rendering and branding.

Can I resell it or hand out copies?

No. You may use it, including at work, but you may not resell or redistribute it. Point people to this site instead. The full terms are in the EULA.

Privacy

Does it upload my screens?

No. Capture, voice and rendering all run on your PC. There is no account, no cloud service and no API key to set up.

Does it connect to the internet?

Not in 2.2.2. Capturing, narrating and rendering make no network connections, and there is no update check or usage reporting. The web recorder opens the sites you choose to record, as a browser would. Versions 2.0.0 to 2.1.2: when the PC was online, the rendering step sent anonymous usage statistics (such as the renderer version, operating system and processor) to the renderer's analytics service, and downloaded fonts from Google Fonts (the font request listed which letters appear in the video, not the text itself). We found no screenshots, narration or project text in either, but it did not match what we promised, so 2.2.2 removes both. Please update.

Our screens contain customer data. Is that a problem?

Everything is processed on the computer. Password, email, phone, card and ID fields are blurred automatically when you capture, and you can blur or redact anything else before the video is rendered. Check the finished video before you share it, as you would any recording.

Is the narrator a real person?

No. All voices are built-in computer voices. In demo mode you can give the narrator a name, and the opening card always says "Narrated by an AI voice".

Can the voice use an AI service?

The built-in voices are offline. If you want AI-written narration, you can export the script, edit it with any AI or writer on your own terms, and import it back.

Using it

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Plan for about 3 GB of free disk space and 8 GB of RAM. There is no Mac or Linux version yet.

What does it capture?

Web apps, where every click becomes a step with the box already placed, and Windows desktop apps, where the clicked control is named for you. You can also import screenshots and short screen clips.

What do I get out?

An MP4 at 720p, 1080p or 9:16 vertical with no watermark, short clips of 15, 30 or 60 seconds, captions as .srt and .vtt, and a step-guide PDF. The Intranet pack adds a poster image and a ready index.html page; the Social pack adds caption and hashtag text.

Which languages can it speak?

The built-in offline voices are English. Captions and the PDF contain whatever text you write.

Why is it so large?

About 1.5 GB installed, because the voice model, renderer and video tools are bundled. That is what lets it work offline.

Can I share a project?

Yes. A project saves as a single .shotreel file you can send to a colleague who has ShotReel.

Contact

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue or email rnetphone1@gmail.com. Teams that want a paid tier or a pilot: tell us what you need, it decides what gets built next.
